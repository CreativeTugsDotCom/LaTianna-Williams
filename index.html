Meet LaTianna

Therapy,

LaTianna Williams is a Licensed Marriage & Family Therapist, Certified Sexologist, and expressive/embodiment practitioner whose work brings together clinical and experiential approaches.

Her work explores the relationship between self, body, sexuality, relationships, and personal expression—creating space for people to better understand themselves and how they connect with others.
